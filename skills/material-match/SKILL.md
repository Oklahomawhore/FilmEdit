---
name: material-match
description: Match local or downloaded material to a specific shot requirement.
---

Follow the task source strategy. Search the provided catalog first in local_first mode, and use web search when the required action is absent. Inspect frames at the actual source interval; use ASR when speech matters. Select on visible action and composition, not the title alone. Record a usable source range. Never reuse an inaccessible asset or claim that a missing shot exists.

The task catalog is not all authorized local material. Use `local_materials` without a sourceId to discover current read grants, then browse directories using the returned sourceId and relativePath. Follow nextOffset until the relevant listing is covered. Import selected files with `import_local_material`; only its returned assetId can be used with frame/asr/beats and in matches. Directory names and filenames are clues, not proof of content. Do not ask users to manually import or bind already authorized files. Browsing does not grant access to parent directories, symlinks, denied paths or other tasks, and it does not publish private files into the shared library.

All matches are checked by an independent reviewer before the editor receives them: visual needs against source frames, audio needs against actual local sound analysis of the selected interval. Use `audio_evidence` for sounds, with explicit start/end source seconds and at most 30 seconds per call; inspect further intervals for longer media. It returns AudioSet predictions, CLAP cosine similarities against a fixed sound vocabulary, signal levels and actual ASR. Similarities are relative comparisons, not probabilities; neither model receives the search request or source title. Consider both models and signal levels, keeping disagreements and unknown sounds explicit. Model scores are not calibrated confidence, unlisted tags do not prove absence, and coarse Music/Vehicle labels do not establish a specific genre/car model. Empty ASR or beat analysis alone is not sound-content evidence. `revisionRequest.checks` reports mismatches or insufficient evidence. Repair those source intervals or report the remaining gap; preserve correct matches and the accepted creative/script requirements. Changing an observation cannot make unchanged source content correct. Evidence-format errors are repaired in the reviewer context, without repeating material search or editing.

In the sound_plan phase, interpret the accepted sound instructions before looking at inventory. Split simultaneous or sequential different sounds into individual requirements; represent silence and absence of music explicitly, without searching for them. In the matching phase, cover each resulting requirement. Finding an engine sound does not also satisfy a separate drum or bird requirement. Reuse an existing matched source when it actually contains several required sounds.

Creative beats and script visualQuery are desired shots, not evidence of existing footage. Material supplementation happens here, after the concept and script: retrieve from the shared library, bind matches, then search/download missing material when the strategy permits. An empty library is not a reason to replace a requested live-action concept with text cards or remove requested sound. Missing material is a sourcing problem; report the exact gap and attempted sources instead of repeatedly rewriting the concept. Cultural background lookup and video search/download are separate tools; a failure in one does not prove the other is unavailable.

Network search explicitly selects YouTube or Bilibili. Choose the source for the requested content and language; record the selected platform in the gap report. If a platform is unreachable, do not repeat equivalent queries there indefinitely or describe every other source as unavailable. Other authorized sources remain separate search decisions; none permits weakening the content requirements.

`download_material` requires an explicit kind: `video` for footage, `audio` for BGM or sound effects. Audio sources may be standalone sound files or video soundtracks; the tool returns an audio-only asset. Use the returned assetId with `audio_evidence` to check the actual sound; ASR and beats additionally help with language and timing. Downloading a soundtrack does not separate speech, music and effects that are mixed together in the source.
