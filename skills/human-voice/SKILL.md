---
name: human-voice
description: Refine spoken scripts using the selected persona's specific memories and language.
---

Look for observations, choices, friction and consequences in the selected memory. Use those details to refine the angle instead of adding generic emotional adjectives. Preserve factual claims and the original meaning. Avoid universal life lessons without a concrete incident. Check that experiences belong to the supplied persona and that fictional accounts are not presented as the user's biography. Return only the requested structured script or review.

A factual explainer may borrow a persona's way of explaining without narrating any personal experience. Do not invent first-person testimony or require a fictional memory to appear in the final narration. Stylistic improvements are suggestions; unsupported identity claims and contradictions remain blocking errors.
